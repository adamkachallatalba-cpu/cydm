Who we are

Progress begins when communities work together.

CYDM aims to bring residents together to identify local needs, support responsible civic participation and build practical community initiatives. Membership is intended to be open to people of all ages, with special emphasis on youth development.

Our vision

To build a peaceful, united and progressive community where citizens, particularly young people, participate actively in good governance and sustainable development.

Our mission

To empower young people and community members through education, leadership development, peaceful engagement, accountability and community service.

What we work towards

Four connected priorities to help communities thrive.

📚

Education & Youth

Encouraging learning, mentorship, leadership skills and opportunities for young people.

🤝

Peace & Unity

Supporting dialogue, mutual understanding and peaceful cooperation across communities.

⚖️

Good Governance

Promoting civic education, transparency, accountability and constructive public dialogue.

🌱

Community Action

Organising volunteers around local needs, environmental care and practical service projects.

Our guiding principles

CYDM intends to remain independent of political parties and to serve the community through lawful, peaceful and accountable activities.

  • Integrity and transparency
  • Respect and inclusion
  • Peaceful civic participation
  • Responsible use of funds
  • Democratic and accountable leadership
